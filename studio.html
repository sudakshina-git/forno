Shared by both viewsBrand rules

The system behind every season.

FORNO is a contemporary European restaurant brand with a warm illustrated world layered in. Eighty per cent editorial restraint, twenty per cent illustrated warmth. Both tools follow these rules.

Wordmark

Painted by hand, never typed.

  • The tomato O is part of the mark; it never stands alone as a logo
  • Clear space on all sides equals the height of the O
  • On photography, only over a dark band or a quiet area
  • Never recoloured, outlined or set in a font
On warm paper
On ink blue

Colour

Paper, ink and one red.

Paper and ink carry the system. Tomato is the accent, used sparingly. Sage and honey support. Red and green never sit at equal weight; that's what keeps FORNO from reading as a generic Italian or Christmas brand.

Seasonal accents

The brand stays. The season changes.

Each season adds three accent colours on top of the core palette. Layout, type and the wordmark don't move.

Typography

One voice for headlines, one for information.

The display face carries warmth and editorial character. The sans carries every price, label and description. No handwritten faces on menus, ever.

Photography

Close enough to taste.

  • Overhead, on dark oak, stoneware plate
  • One warm side light, deep shadow
  • Blistered crust, real imperfection
  • No props louder than the food

Applications

Everything the customer touches.

  • Kraft, not white: the box is warm before it's opened
  • The checkerboard stays on the sides, never the lid
  • Wordmark and rider on the lid; nothing else
  • The tomato pattern is for paper that gets thrown away

Mascot

One rider. Used sparingly.

  • A pizza slice on a bicycle, red knitted hat and scarf
  • Packaging, cards and small moments, never the interface
  • Four poses, no family of characters
  • Moves only in small reactions

How FORNO writes

Short, specific, a little dry.

Every caption, poster line and menu description uses the same tone. The left column is how FORNO writes; the right is the generic restaurant copy it avoids.

FORNO writes

  • Duck. Braised red cabbage. On a pizza. We're sorry. We're not sorry.
  • It's minus two. The bike doesn't mind.
  • Three days cold. Twelve hours out. Ninety seconds in.

FORNO never writes

  • Warmth is waiting for you this festive season.
  • Indulge in our delicious holiday treats!
  • Pizza for a brighter day.
